How it works

Submit
Students submit their work individually or as part of a group.
Review
Peerofessor assigns students or groups to review one another’s work using the evaluation criteria provided for the assignment.
Receive feedback
Students receive scores and written feedback from their peers and can follow their progress across assignments.
For students

Learn by reviewing,
not only by submitting

Peerofessor makes peer evaluation part of the learning process. Students see how others approach the same task, assess work using shared criteria, and receive feedback on their own submissions.

For educators

Organize peer evaluation
without managing every pairing by hand

Create classes and assignments, organize students into groups, define evaluation rounds and criteria, and keep an overview of submissions and evaluations.

Peerofessor handles the assignment of peer reviews while educators retain control over the course setup.

Peerofessor

A simple platform for
structured peer review

Peerofessor is being developed as a tool for courses and projects in which students learn both by producing their own work and by evaluating the work of others.
